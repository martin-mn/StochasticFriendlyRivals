## The switches (`wfc.c`; `diff -u ref/wfc_prod.c wfc.c` = `ref/wfc_vs_prod.diff`)

The diff is 1 changed line (`EPS = 1e-4` → `EPS = EPSV`, with `#define EPSV 1e-4` by default) and insertions that
are comments or inside `#ifdef`. `compile.sh` refuses to build unless the preprocessed default program, at F1 and
F2 and with the defaults spelled out (`-DEPSV=1e-4`, `-DBETAV=100.0`), is token for token `ref/wfc_prod.c`
(whose sha256 it checks), so the same compiler emits the same machine code.

* **`-DEPSV=<ε>`**: the pair chain's error rate; changes only the clamped vectors of the dynamics.
* **`-DLOCALMUT`** (K0): `sn[i]=newmutant()` becomes `sn[i]=newmutant_local(s[i])`. The mutant copies its parent and
  changes one coordinate j (uniform): with probability ½ it is redrawn from the α-law, otherwise a free p_j takes a
  N(0, 0.1²) step folded into (0,1) (a fold landing on 0 or 1 is redrawn, so a free coordinate never becomes
  pinned) and a pinned p_j is left alone. A no-op, or a redraw that returns the same value, keeps the parent's
  strategy (no new strategy, no imitation that generation). The initial population is α-law draws as in
  production. Both moves leave the α-law invariant, so production's kernel masses stay the neutral baseline.
  `-DNUGLOB=ν` (optional, default 0, no random number spent) makes a fraction ν of mutants global draws.
  **The exact W friendly-rival counter under K0.** A local kernel grows clouds of distinct friendly rivals in W
  (at ipt 466, 1521 distinct ones within 2 × 10⁵ generations), each needing a full 65536-co-player scan to certify,
  which would cost hours per replicate. K0 therefore runs production's verdict (no binary co-player with
  W_DC − W_CD < −10⁻⁹) with recent refuters tried first, counts every pair evaluation, and stops testing small
  candidates (AB < 10⁶) once 10⁸ evaluations (≈ 2 min) are spent in a replicate; their individual-generations go
  to `ex111u`. So `ex111 ≤ true ≤ ex111 + ex111u`, exact when `ex111u = 0` (where friendly rivals are rare, as in
  production), and where a cloud holds the population the sampled share `a111` (4000 samples) measures it; the
  analysis uses the exact count where `ex111u = 0` and the sampled share otherwise. A `MUT` line per replicate
  gives the move counts, the full scans, the evaluations and `ex111u`. `-DK0VERIFY` (validation only) also runs
  production's test on every candidate and stops at the first disagreement.
* **`-DTSERIES`** (TS): observation only. Mean payoff in `TS_NB` (400) blocks over the whole run; the exact W
  friendly-rival individual-generations per block (every candidate is tested at death, as production does for the
  second half); `TS_NI` (10) individuals at each of `TS_NT` (2000) evenly spaced times, drawn by a **second
  xoshiro256** stream** seeded `seed ^ 0x5453455249455332`. The main stream is never touched, so the trajectory,
  the REP line and the S dump are production's; the TS strategies are classified after the REP line with the
  classifier's state (`n111checked`, the Nash-violator list, the scan counters) saved and restored. Output: `TB`,
  `TS`, `TSR` lines in the r_ file, `T_*.bin` (distinct TS strategies, S-dump format) and `T_*.bin.k` (per sampled
  individual, the index of its strategy). At 4 × 10⁷ generations the REP line is the second half [2×10⁷, 4×10⁷];
  the production window [5×10⁶, 10⁷] is read from the blocks and samples.
* **`-DBETAV=0.0`** (B0) is production's own macro: every imitation has probability ½.
* α is production's runtime argument (`1/4`, `2/5` are exact rationals).

## Analysis (`analyze.py`, local, seconds)

    python3 analyze.py --selftest          # production F1 through the same code: 61 undetermined, median 0.987, ... (passes)
    python3 analyze.py A1 A2 K0 E3 E5 E3F2 E5F2 B0 B0K0 TS
Per arm and per wedge, in the paper's format: median efficiency; the most abundant atom (games led by 111 and by
110, and which others); median friendly-rival and competitive shares; median 111 enrichment; the most enriched
atom where the ten replicates determine it at 95 %; games at which all ten replicates name the leading atom. The
same table for production at the same games, the binary (α = ½) medians, and the changes: games whose leading atom
moved (with k-of-10 replicate agreement on both sides), efficiency changes with Welch t, and whether the arm's W, N,
E medians fall inside production's bootstrap-over-replicates 95 % intervals. `analysis/<arm>_games.tsv` has one row
per game. B0: for every atom at every game, whether the t interval of the ten replicate shares contains the kernel
mass, and the neutral variance ratio. TS: per game, efficiency and leading-atom share in the production window P
and the second half L against production; Q3-vs-Q4 z (blocks and 10 batch means); Geyer ESS; friendly-rival
takeover and escape rates per 10⁷ generations with Poisson intervals.

## Success criteria (evaluated and printed by `analyze.py`)

* **A1, A2, K0** (report 5): 111 leads and is the determined most enriched atom at ≥ 15 of the 16 S games; median
  111 enrichment in N and E at least 10× below the binary 30 and 170; 110 most enriched outside S where determined;
  median efficiency at least the binary's at the same games. A monotone drift toward the binary values as α rises
  to 2/5 is itself a result.
* **E3, E5, E3F2, E5F2** (report 6, as in the binary paper [PR2026]): leading atom unchanged at ≥ 95 % of games; efficiency
  within replicate spread; friendly rivals still lead every S game at N = 1000; W/N/E friendly-rival and competitive
  medians inside production's bootstrap intervals. At N = 100, ε = 10⁻³ (Nβε = 0.3) the S games whose leader moves
  are listed (the binary paper reports such moves from 110 to 111).
* **B0**: every atom's replicate interval contains its kernel mass at 8 of 8 games, for both kernels.
* **TS**: at every unsplit game the quarters agree within 2 SE and ESS ≥ 50 per replicate for the leading-atom
  share; the leading atom and efficiency agree between P and L within replicate spread; at split and takeover
  games, whether the friendly-rival state persists, with rates.
